# MKG 90-day content program

Owner: Miami Knife Guy. Editorial source: Sean's approved service rules, photographed work, and answers to real customer questions.

## Cadence

Publish two substantial pieces per month for three months. Each month pairs one practical answer with one documented first-party job or process story. Do not publish a job story without the date, knife type, starting condition, work performed, result, photographs, and Sean's approval. Obtain permission before using customer names or identifying details.

| Month | Practical answer | First-party story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Local pickup price and handoff (published) | A real repair or sharpening job with before/after photos (needs Sean's job notes) |
| 2 | Restaurant last-ticket-to-prep handoff (published) | Restaurant fleet or steak-knife cycle with documented workflow (needs client permission where identifiable) |
| 3 | Premium knife assessment: sharpening, thinning, or repair | Mail-in order from quote through return with real timestamps and packing photos |

The first two answer articles are published early to establish a working template. Replace the month-1/month-2 slots above only after choosing the next six-week publication dates.

## Per-piece workflow

1. Capture a real customer question or job in the Growth Engine content pipeline. Record the related service offer and source.
2. Have Sean check all facts, prices, turnaround language, and service area limits. Gather original photos and consent for identifiable clients.
3. Draft a direct answer in the opening paragraph, then the steps, decision criteria, examples, and limitations. Avoid invented case facts and generic AI summaries.
4. Link to the relevant service page, request form, proof gallery, and related guide. Every CTA must route to a form that records source, service type, page, and attribution in the CRM.
5. Check title, unique description, canonical URL, Article schema, image alt text, mobile readability, and sitemap entry. Publish.
6. Review Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools monthly for indexed pages, impressions, search queries, click-through rate, and crawl issues. Review the Growth Engine for request volume and actual booked work from content. Update pieces when price or policy changes.

## Search and answer-engine approach

Use clear question headings and concise answers; cite first-hand observations and documented work. Keep structured data factual and consistent with the visible page. Search and AI visibility come from crawlable, helpful, original content and sound technical SEO. Do not add FAQ markup merely to chase rich results. Track AI referral sources where analytics exposes them, but do not treat citation counts as a substitute for qualified leads.

## Open inputs

Sean's first approved repair case with photos; any identifiable client permissions; access to Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools if not already connected; monthly editorial review date.
