Technical Authority

17 years of precision.

A true edge isn't just ground — it's coaxed from the heavy steel of Western workhorses to the delicate, single-bevel geometry of Japanese cutlery.

Starting in San Francisco's finest kitchens and now serving South Florida's most demanding hospitality teams, MKG has built a practice around one principle: restore the mechanical integrity that defines a high-performance tool.

I don't just sharpen. I restore the soul of the blade.
